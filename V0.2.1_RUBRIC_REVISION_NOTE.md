# TrialDiff v0.2.1 Rubric Revision Note

Status: rubric-boundary revision for fresh re-certification
Date: 2026-06-18

This note records the v0.2.1 revision to `SEVERITY_RUBRIC_v0.2.md`.

The revision is based on `V0.2.1_DISAGREEMENT_ANALYSIS.md`, which identified
localized high/medium rubric gaps before any fresh re-certification sample was
drawn. The revised rubric must be frozen by commit and cited by hash in any
v0.2.1 calibration report.

## Changed Boundaries

1. Active-recruitment timeline slips.
   - Approximately one-year active-recruitment timeline slips are generally
     medium unless paired with stronger substantive signals.
   - Very large active-recruitment slips, on the order of two years or more, may
     be high by magnitude alone.

2. Secondary-outcome additions.
   - Routine exploratory, correlative, biomarker, or follow-up secondary outcome
     additions before completion are generally medium.
   - Secondary additions become high when central to efficacy, safety, or
     decision-relevant interpretation.

3. Results posting and historical backfill.
   - Outcome text edits co-incident with `/hasResults`, `/resultsSection`, or
     large results population are presumptive results reconciliation unless an
     independent substantive design or endpoint change is visible.
   - Completed-record backfill or historical migration is not critical unless it
     visibly removes or redefines information needed to interpret the completed
     trial.

4. Serious adverse events inside first results posting.
   - Serious adverse event additions/modifications inside first results posting
     are high only when they create a review-priority question beyond routine
     results population.
   - Serious adverse event removals remain high or critical depending on
     context.

## Independence Constraint

These changes are rubric-boundary clarifications, not classifier-pass tuning.

They should be evaluated on fresh records, not by re-scoring the v0.2 diagnostic
sample as proof of success.

## Certification Constraint

This revised rubric does not unblock the buyer-facing brief.

The brief remains blocked until revised classifier output is re-certified on a
fresh blinded package with records outside the v0.2 diagnostic sample.
