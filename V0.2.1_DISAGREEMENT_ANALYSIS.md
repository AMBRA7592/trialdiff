## 1. Labeling Procedure

Each reviewer disagreement is labeled against the frozen rubric, not against
TrialDiff's desired pass rate.

Primary labels:

- `rubric_gap`: the rubric is silent, underspecified, or internally tense for
  the relevant change-type/timing combination.
- `judgment_variance`: the rubric gives a usable standard, but the record sits
  near a boundary where reasonable reviewers can differ.
- `reviewer_error`: one reviewer appears to contradict a clear rubric criterion
  or miss a material visible fact.

Rubric anchors used most often:

- Results reconciliation: outcome-field text changes co-incident with
  `/hasResults` or `/resultsSection` are presumptively low unless a substantive
  independent change is visible.
- Critical: primary evidence interpretation, terminal-status interpretability,
  late-stage interpretability removal, or similarly high-stakes changes.
- High: consequential but less determinative design, intervention, population,
  status, secondary outcome, or late-stage timeline changes.
- Medium: substantive but routine, expected, lower consequence, or not unusually
  timed.
- Low: real but unlikely to affect interpretation.

No record is labeled `reviewer_error` in this pass. Several records may contain
reviewer mistakes, but the visible evidence was not clean enough to discount a
reviewer without third adjudication. The safer diagnostic label is therefore
`rubric_gap` or `judgment_variance`.

## 4. Rule Fix List From Agreements

These fixes are supported by reviewer agreement and do not depend on resolving
the high/medium disagreement first:

1. Tighten `critical`.
   - Critical should require clear primary-evidence reinterpretation, terminal
     interpretability failure, endpoint/result removal after terminal status, or
     similarly high-stakes review value.

2. Down-tier most `timeline_significant_shift`.
   - Timeline shifts should not become high solely from date movement unless
     late-stage, completion-proximate, post-completion, or independently extreme.

3. Suppress results-reconciliation false positives.
   - Outcome changes co-incident with `/hasResults` or `/resultsSection` should
     be low unless an independent substantive protocol/outcome change is visible.

4. Split `whyStopped`.
   - Missing, null, empty, or placeholder-only explanation may remain critical.
   - Present but business-like or low-specificity explanation should generally
     be high.

## 5. Rubric Work Before High-Tier Rule Changes

The following high/medium boundaries should be clarified from review-priority
principle, blind to their effect on TrialDiff's pass rate:

1. Does a very large completion-date slip during active recruiting become high
   by magnitude alone?
2. Is a 12-month recruiting-stage slip high, or medium unless late-stage?
3. Are routine/exploratory secondary-outcome additions before completion high,
   or medium unless they alter trial interpretability?
4. Are serious/adverse-event results added inside first results posting high, or
   low as results reconciliation?
5. How should historical backfill/migration of completed records be separated
   from post-completion substantive amendment?

Any revised rubric text must be committed under a new hash before re-scoring or
re-calibration.

## 6. Certification Constraint

The v0.2 sample is now fully unblinded inside the repository. It can diagnose
rule/rubric changes but cannot certify them.

Re-certification requires:

- fresh critical records from corpus expansion;
- the 21 unused high records from the 25-study alpha reserved as a high holdout;
- fresh medium and low/admin records where available;
- a blinded review package that does not expose crosswalk fields;
- a new `rule_set_hash` for revised classifier output;
- a new rubric hash if the rubric is revised.

The buyer-facing brief remains blocked until re-certification passes on a
non-tuned sample.
